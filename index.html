Brand Discovery Workshop

Building a brand
from the inside out.

Work through each exercise with your client. Fill in answers collaboratively, then generate structured brand insights to fuel your strategy.

01 Origin Story The Founding Moment ▾
Where did this all begin? Tell the story of how this brand came to exist — the moment, the place, the person.
02 Founder Frustration The Breaking Point ▾
What made the founder say 'enough — someone needs to fix this'? This frustration is the emotional engine of the brand.
03 Customer Switch The Tipping Point ▾
Why do customers leave their current solution and come to this brand? What is the moment they decide enough is enough?
04 Customer Hero The Transformation ▾
The brand is not the hero — the customer is. This exercise defines the transformation the customer goes through.
05 Category Redefinition Rewriting the Rules ▾
Great brands don't just compete in a category — they redefine it. What new game is this brand playing?
06 Onlyness The Only Statement ▾
Complete this statement until it is so specific it could only describe this brand. Vague onlyness is not onlyness.
07 Industry Enemy The Villain ▾
Every strong brand stands against something. Name the enemy — not a competitor, but a bad practice, broken belief, or industry norm.
08 We Believe… Core Convictions ▾
These are the brand's non-negotiable beliefs. Principles it would never compromise on, even at commercial cost.
09 Brand As a Person Personality Mapping ▾
Imagine the brand as a fully realized human being. Give them a life, a personality, a way of showing up in the world.
10 Brand Metaphor The Brand Image ▾
A great metaphor captures in one image what takes paragraphs to explain. Find the image that feels undeniably right.
11 Brand Temperature Energy & Tone ▾
Position the brand honestly between each pair. There is no correct answer — find where this brand authentically lives.
12 Customer Pain The Deep Frustration ▾
Go beneath the surface complaint. There's always a deeper fear, identity threat, or unmet need underneath what customers say.
13 Differentiation The Brand's Edge ▾
Be brutally honest. 'We care more' and 'we're passionate' are not differentiators. What is provably, specifically different?
14 Future Headline The North Star ▾
Write the headline this brand wants to earn. Not a mission statement — a real newspaper headline, 5 years from now.
15 Positioning The Strategic Statement ▾
Draft the positioning statement now, before the AI refines it. Rough is fine — the act of writing it surfaces assumptions.
